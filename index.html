FAQ

Questions & answers

How do online lessons work?

Lessons take place on Zoom or Google Meet. All you need is a phone, tablet or computer with internet. Your teacher shares materials on screen and sends notes after each lesson.

How much is the trial lesson?

The trial lesson is 15 minutes and costs $5. It's a relaxed way to meet your teacher, check your level and plan your lessons — with no obligation to continue.

What level do I need?

Every level is welcome — from complete beginners to advanced learners. We check your level during the trial lesson.

Can I study with a native English speaker?

Yes. Our team includes native English-speaking teachers. Just tell us your preference when you book.
